Ethics

Responsible disclosure.

Coordinated disclosure policy

Every finding follows coordinated disclosure. Each candidate is reproduced in an isolated local WordPress lab, deduplicated against the public NVD / Patchstack catalogues to avoid re-reporting known issues, and submitted through WPScan for triage.

Affected plugin names, versions, endpoints, and any proof-of-concept detail are deliberately withheld until vendors ship a fix and identifiers are assigned — publishing them earlier would hand out a live exploitation roadmap. Beyond the single published CVE, no identifiers have been issued from this campaign yet, so those findings are listed here only by vulnerability class, severity, and access level.

Confirmed disclosures are tracked on the Wordfence researcher profile, and verified submissions are visible on the WPScan leaderboard as they publish.
